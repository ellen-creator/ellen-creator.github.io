Strong ad traffic, but fewer than 1% of visitors asked for help: people opened the chatbot and left. Interviews showed two opposite visitors, panicked victims who needed reassurance first and researchers who needed technical proof. I split one generic journey into two tracks, rewrote the first chatbot message ("Don't blame yourself. It's not your fault."), renamed CTAs after the action, and added a fixed one-step inquiry bar.

**Lead quality +42%, chatbot completion 68%, CTA click-through +55%.**
