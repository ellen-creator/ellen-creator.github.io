People with ADHD or autism often notice too late that their energy has run out, and most trackers punish them with streaks and numbers. I designed LUMI end to end: two user groups, a day-shaped user flow, four Lumi characters that grow on the days you log, a calm two-hue palette chosen from sensory-overload research, and four tabs that each answer one question (Today, Journal, Analysis, Energizer).

AI was part of every stage: AI image generation to explore characters, Claude Code to build and iterate the SwiftUI iPhone + Watch app, and Figma to rebuild every shipped screen as editable layers.

**Now in beta with 25 potential users, testing data accuracy and usability.**
