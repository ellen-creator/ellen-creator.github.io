I ordered from the same restaurant on DoorDash and Uber Eats and audited every step against 10 cognitive concepts (University of Michigan, SI 588). Five fixes target DoorDash directly: an "Options +" button that says what it will do, a one-tap Delivery | Pickup toggle, a "Reorder your favourites" row of dish photos, a delay banner that gives a reason and a new ETA, and a sticky bar with the running total.

Each idea went from a pen sketch to a before/after wireframe, with the trade-off it creates and the metric I would test. Concept work, not affiliated with DoorDash.
