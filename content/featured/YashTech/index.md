A major TV home-shopping company was losing buyers as they moved from TV to mobile; the app was rated 2.8. We segmented customers from purchase and behaviour data, built a targeting framework for each purchase stage (awareness, consider, purchase), and proposed UI for each segment: price-focused reviews for comparers, social proof and bundles in the cart, and a high-contrast, fewer-tap UI for the 50–60 buyers who drove the most sales.

**After the renewal the app reached 4.8/5 on Google Play (2023), up from 2.8 (2021).**
